Our story

Built Around My Favorites

JPKicks started with a simple idea: you don't need dozens of brands to build a great shoe collection. Yeezy, New Balance, and Cole Haan are three of my personal favorites, and I believe they cover everything a well-rounded collection needs.

Yeezys bring distinctive designs that make an outfit stand out. New Balance offers classic sneakers that are easy to wear every day. Cole Haan rounds out the collection with polished styles for occasions that call for something a little dressier.

I created JPKicks to bring those favorites together in one place. Every pair in the collection reflects my own taste and the belief that great shoes should earn their place in your rotation.

Our mission

Fewer Brands. More Possibilities.

Our mission is to make building a versatile shoe collection simple by bringing together carefully selected styles from three brands we love. We want to help you find shoes that express your personality and fit the way you live.
